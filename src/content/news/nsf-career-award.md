---
title: "Yu Zhang receives NSF CAREER Award"
date: 2021-03-15
summary: "Lab director Yu Zhang was recognized with a National Science Foundation CAREER Award, the agency's most competitive award for early-career faculty."
---

Lab director Yu "Tony" Zhang has received a CAREER Award from the National Science Foundation, its most competitive recognition for early-career faculty who exemplify the role of teacher-scholar through outstanding research and education. The award supports the lab's continuing work on human-aware planning and decision-making for cooperative robotic systems.
