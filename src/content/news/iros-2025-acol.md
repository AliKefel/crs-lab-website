summary: "\"ACoL: From Abstractions to Grounded Languages for Robust Coordination of Task Planning Robots\" was accepted to IROS 2025."
---

A new paper from the lab, "ACoL: From Abstractions to Grounded Languages for Robust Coordination of Task Planning Robots," has been accepted to the IEEE/RSJ International Conference on Intelligent Robots and Systems (IROS) 2025. The work addresses how robots can move between abstract task representations and grounded language to coordinate more robustly during planning.
