title: "Two papers accepted to ICRA 2026"
date: 2026-01-30
summary: "\"Safe Explicable Policy Search\" and \"Assigning Multi-Robot Tasks to Multitasking Robots\" were both accepted to ICRA 2026."
---

The lab will present two papers at the IEEE International Conference on Robotics and Automation (ICRA) 2026: "Safe Explicable Policy Search," on planning robot behavior that stays both safe and interpretable to human observers, and "Assigning Multi-Robot Tasks to Multitasking Robots," on task allocation for robots that can carry more than one job at a time.
