The lab will present two papers at the IEEE International Conference on Robotics and Automation (ICRA) 2026: "Safe Explicable Policy Search," on planning robot behavior that stays both safe and interpretable to human observers, and "Assigning Multi-Robot Tasks to Multitasking Robots," on task allocation for robots that can carry more than one job at a time. Separately, lab director Yu Zhang has taken on an Associate Editor role for IROS 2026.
