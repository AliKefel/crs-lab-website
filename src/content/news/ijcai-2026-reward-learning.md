Congratulations to Taha Shaheen: the lab's paper analyzing a critical pitfall in reward learning from human feedback has been accepted to the International Joint Conference on Artificial Intelligence (IJCAI) 2026. The work provides empirical evidence for a failure mode that affects how learned reward models generalize, with implications for training robots and agents from human preference data.
