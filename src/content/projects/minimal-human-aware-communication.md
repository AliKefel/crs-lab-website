Not every robot action needs to be explained, and not every explanation needs words. This project investigates communication modalities beyond conventional dialogue interfaces, asking what information is actually necessary for a human teammate to stay coordinated with a robot, and developing minimal, context-sensitive communication strategies, including visual and spatial cues, that stay informative without becoming a distraction.
