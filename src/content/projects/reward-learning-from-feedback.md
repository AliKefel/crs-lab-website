title: "Reward Learning from Human Feedback"
summary: "Studying how robots learn preferences from human feedback, and the failure modes that arise when that feedback is biased or sparse."
thrust: learning

---

Robots that learn from human feedback need to adapt to preferences that are often expressed indirectly, inconsistently, or with only partial information. This project studies reward learning and reward transfer techniques for sequential decision-making, including empirical work identifying failure modes in reward models trained from human feedback, toward mutual learning that supports fluent long-term collaboration.
